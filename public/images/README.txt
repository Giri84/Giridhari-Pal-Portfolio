Drop a real photo here as `avatar.jpg` (a square image, at least 560x560px,
works best) if you want to replace the generated monogram in the hero section.

To wire it up:
1. Add your photo to this folder as `avatar.jpg`.
2. Open `components/SignalAvatar.jsx`.
3. Replace the "GP" monogram block with a Next.js <Image> tag pointing at
   `/images/avatar.jpg` (an example is commented at the bottom of that file).
